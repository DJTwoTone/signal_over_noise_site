## Editorial plan

- **Core argument:** Before commenting on polish, check whether the evidence earns the scale of the commitment the slide asks for.
- **Example:** A synthetic proposal claims a new handoff process will eliminate review delays. The process notes show late reviewer assignment; they may justify a limited trial, but do not establish the effect of a company-wide rollout.
- **Reader check:** What commitment is requested? Which parts of the case are observed and which predict an outcome? What smaller step would reduce uncertainty?
- **Outline:** Identify the requested commitment; show a labelled synthetic proposal; separate observed facts from predicted results; right-size the next step; invite a self-check; diagnostic CTA.
- **Internal links:** Link to *Why Good Presenters Still Bury the Ask* for the separate question of what the room is being asked to do. Link to *An Accurate Chart Can Still Overstate Its Case* for the narrower task of checking a chart’s headline against its measure.
- **Repurposing:** LinkedIn before/after post using the same clearly labelled synthetic slide. Prepare only after the article is finalized and its canonical URL is live.
