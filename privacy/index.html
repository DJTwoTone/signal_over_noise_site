Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Signal over Noise collects only the information needed to respond to inquiries, deliver resources, review presentation materials, and provide presentation support.

This page explains what we collect, how we use it, and how you can ask us to update or delete information you have shared.

Information we collect

When you use this site or submit a form, we may collect:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your role, organization, or team context.
  • Details about the presentation, workshop, or support request.
  • Audience information, deadlines, goals, and current challenges.
  • Scripts, slide decks, delivery recordings, uploaded files, or shared links you choose to submit.
  • Workshop inquiry details such as participant count, format, timing, and main support needs.
  • Basic technical and routing information, such as the page you submitted from, referral page, campaign parameters, and which call-to-action led to the form.

How we use information

We use the information you submit to:

  • Respond to your inquiry.
  • Send requested resources, such as the Presenter Toolkit.
  • Review Free Presentation Diagnostic requests.
  • Recommend the most useful next step or support scope.
  • Plan workshops or training sessions.
  • Communicate about presentation support.
  • Improve the site, forms, and visitor routes.
  • Keep basic business records.

Presentation materials and uploaded files

If you submit a script, slide deck, delivery recording, presentation link, or related material, we use it to review your request, prepare feedback, plan support, or respond to your inquiry.

We do not publish, sell, or share submitted presentation materials as public examples without permission.

Please do not submit highly confidential, regulated, or sensitive information unless you are comfortable sharing it for review.

Forms and service providers

We use third-party tools to operate the site and handle requests. These may include:

  • Tally for form submissions.
  • Google Workspace for email and communication.
  • Cloudflare for domain and DNS services.
  • Website hosting services, such as GitHub Pages or another static hosting provider, for serving the site.
  • Analytics or routing tools to understand how visitors use the site.

These providers may process information on our behalf so we can operate the website, receive inquiries, send replies, and improve the service.

Routing and analytics information

Our forms may collect basic routing information such as:

  • The page where the form was submitted.
  • The type of request.
  • The call-to-action that led to the form.
  • Campaign or UTM parameters.
  • Referral page.
  • Workshop follow-up status.

We use this information to understand which pages, campaigns, and request routes are working. This helps us improve the site and respond to inquiries more effectively.

Sharing information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the website, forms, email, analytics, scheduling, or client communication. We may also share information if required by law or necessary to protect our rights, safety, or business operations.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and diagnostic submissions only as long as needed to respond, provide support, keep business records, or improve the service.

We aim to review old form submissions and submitted materials every 12 months and delete information that is no longer needed.

Access, correction, and deletion requests

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information you submitted.

To make a request, email privacy@signal-over-noise.coach.

We may need to verify your identity or ask for details about the request before making changes.

Children and students

This site is intended for professional, educational, and organizational presentation support. It is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a minor has submitted personal information that should be removed, contact us at privacy@signal-over-noise.coach.

Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page with the updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, access requests, correction requests, or deletion requests, email privacy@signal-over-noise.coach.

For general inquiries, email hello@signal-over-noise.coach.
