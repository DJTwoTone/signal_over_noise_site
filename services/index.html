Pricing and Scope FAQ

A few practical pricing and scope questions.

How much does it cost?

Most direct clients start with either a focused coaching/review session at ₩150,000 or a Presentation Sprint from ₩375,000. Script, slide, and deck work are priced by scope because length, complexity, and deadline matter. Team workshops start at ₩1,000,000. Partner-hosted programs are priced separately by Mallang Lingo Club.

Why start with the free diagnostic?

Because buying the wrong kind of help is annoying and expensive. The diagnostic helps identify whether the real issue is the message, script, slides, delivery, timeline, or team context. From there, we recommend the smallest useful next step.

When should I skip the free diagnostic and request paid support?

Request paid support when you already know you need help, the presentation is important, or the deadline is close. The free diagnostic is best when the problem is unclear.

What changes the price?

Scope, urgency, material length, number of slides, amount of rewriting, rehearsal needs, and whether the work is for one person or a team.

Do you do full deck builds or script rewrites?

Yes, when the scope calls for it. The free diagnostic may identify that need, but full builds and rewrites are paid support.

Can you help if my deadline is close?

Usually, yes, but the scope may need to be smaller. A tight deadline is often better for focused fixes than a full rebuild.

Do you work on scripts and slides together?

Yes. That is often the point. A presentation usually breaks when the message, deck, and delivery are treated as separate problems.

Are partner classes priced the same as direct coaching?

No. Partner classes are priced and administered by Mallang Lingo Club. Direct coaching is more focused, more flexible, and built around a specific presentation or communication challenge.
