What kind of help do you need?

Presentation problems usually show up in one of six places. Use this as a quick guide, or start with the diagnostic if you are unsure.

Problem

The message is unclear.

Best fit: Message Refinement, Script Writing, or Presentation Sprint

Problem

The deck feels crowded, uneven, or hard to follow.

Best fit: Slide Refinement or Deck Building

Problem

The content is close, but delivery needs work.

Best fit: Presentation Rehearsal or Coaching

Problem

The presentation is soon and needs fast improvement.

Best fit: Presentation Sprint

Problem

You want to build long-term presentation skill.

Best fit: Ongoing Presentation Coaching

Problem

Your team needs shared presentation standards.

Best fit: Team Workshops

Pricing FAQ

A few practical pricing questions.

How much does it cost?

Most direct clients start with either a focused coaching/review session at ₩180,000 or a Presentation Sprint from ₩450,000. Script, slide, and deck work are priced by scope because length, complexity, and deadline matter. Team workshops start at ₩1,200,000. Partner-hosted programs are priced separately by Mallang Lingo Club.

Why start with the free diagnostic?

Because buying the wrong kind of help is annoying and expensive. The diagnostic helps identify whether the real issue is the message, script, slides, delivery, timeline, or team context. From there, we recommend the smallest useful next step.

Are partner classes priced the same as direct coaching?

No. Partner classes and direct coaching solve different problems. Partner classes are hosted and administered by Mallang Lingo Club. Direct coaching is more focused, more flexible, and built around a specific presentation or communication challenge.
