# Pricing Research Notes

Last updated: May 1, 2026

## Recommended Starting Prices

These are recommended public starting prices for the homepage, based on current public benchmarks for deck review, presentation design, presentation coaching, and workshop support.

| Offer | Recommended public starting price | Why |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Presentation Diagnostic | Starting at $750 | Fits between low-cost deck feedback and premium investor-deck audits. It signals founder-led judgment without promising full redesign or coaching. |
| Presentation Sprint | Starting at $2,500 | Matches the low end of strategy-led deck support and high-stakes presentation preparation, while staying below full agency pitch-deck development. |
| Presentation Coaching | Starting at $1,200/month | Supports a premium but accessible monthly coaching path, roughly equivalent to 4 sessions plus light async review. |

Do not publish a full price menu yet. The homepage should show starting prices and route serious buyers into a diagnostic or call, because scope varies by deck condition, turnaround, rehearsal needs, and stakeholder risk.

## Market Benchmarks

### Diagnostic / review work

- Lightweight pitch-deck reviews can be very low priced, including examples around $149 for a limited deck review.
- More serious deck reviews cluster around $400-$1,000.
- Premium or investor-focused audits can reach $1,500-$2,500, especially when they include slide-by-slide feedback and a walkthrough call.
- Signal over Noise should not compete with commodity slide feedback. The diagnostic is a strategic review across message, deck, and delivery, so $750 is a reasonable entry price. If demand is strong, move the diagnostic to $950 or $1,250.

### Sprint / deck support

- Basic or commodity deck design can start below $1,000, but it is usually positioned as design execution.
- Agency-level 10-slide decks commonly land around $1,500-$4,000.
- Startup pitch-deck services and strategy-led deck projects often run from $1,500 to $10,000+.
- A Signal over Noise sprint should be priced as presentation improvement, not slide production. Starting at $2,500 is a defensible floor for focused message/deck/delivery work. More complex or urgent projects should quote at $3,500-$6,000.

### Coaching

- Public speaking and presentation coaching often appears between $150 and $350 per hour for individual providers.
- Premium presentation coaching can run $275-$375 for a single hour and $2,500+ for 10-session packages.
- Broader presentation-coach marketplace guidance puts traditional coaching at roughly $125-$525 per hour, with full engagements from $500 to $10,000+.
- A monthly coaching price should avoid looking like generic hourly tutoring. Starting at $1,200/month is a credible entry point if it includes recurring sessions plus light async material review.

### Workshops

- Public presentation-skills workshop pricing commonly starts around $1,500-$3,500 for short or half-day formats.
- Full-day workshops can run from roughly $1,695 to $5,000+ depending on customization, audience level, and participant count.
- Keep workshops custom-priced for now. If a public anchor is needed later, use "Workshops from $3,500" or "Private workshops from $5,000" depending on desired buyer profile.
