# Deck Building Intake

Use this short intake to give Ben enough context to scope a deck-building engagement. Required questions establish the audience, outcome, and timing. Everything else is optional; you can add detail later.

## Required

### 1. Who is the audience?

Who will see this presentation? Include roles, organization type, language, and anything important about their context.

### 2. What should the audience decide or do?

Describe the decision, approval, action, or change you want the presentation to drive.

### 3. When is the presentation date or deadline?

Include the final delivery date, rehearsal date, or any milestones that affect the schedule.

## Optional — send what you have

- Source data, research, evidence, or reference documents
- Existing script, outline, story, or speaker notes
- Existing slides or deck files
- Brand guidance, style references, or examples of work you like
- Logos, images, diagrams, charts, screenshots, or other assets
- Required format, slide count, platform, or technical constraints
- Stakeholder feedback, known objections, or approval requirements
- Language, localization, accessibility, or confidentiality requirements
- Anything that currently feels unclear, weak, or risky

## Sharing guidance

Send only material you are permitted to share. Use a redacted or partial version, or provide a share link with appropriate access controls, when the source material is sensitive. An NDA is available on request. Request forms are provided by Tally; review its privacy policy before submitting confidential material.

## What happens next

Ben reviews the intake and follows up with scope, timing, and a quote. Deck Building starts from ₩1,200,000; the final quote depends on the material and agreed scope.
