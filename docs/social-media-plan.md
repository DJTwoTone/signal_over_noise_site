# Signal over Noise Social Media Plan

## Goal

Use social channels to make the diagnostic-first offer easier to understand: show real presentation problems, visible improvements, and practical guidance that points back to the free diagnostic.

## Channel Roles

| Channel | Role | Recommended cadence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | Primary business channel for founders, operators, academics, and professional teams. | 2-3 posts per week |
| YouTube | Longer proof walkthroughs, diagnostic explainers, and presentation teardown clips. | 1-2 videos per month |
| Instagram | Visual before/after clips, slide cleanup examples, and short rehearsal tips. | 2 posts or reels per week |
| TikTok | Short teaching clips, high-friction presentation mistakes, and quick fixes. | 2-3 clips per week if capacity allows |
| Facebook | Basic brand presence and reposting for people who discover the business there. | Cross-post selectively |
| X / Threads | Lightweight commentary, presentation principles, and links to proof/examples. | Optional; use only if it does not dilute LinkedIn |

## Content Pillars

1. Diagnostic proof: before/after slides, annotated script excerpts, and "what changed" posts.
2. Presentation clarity: short lessons on message hierarchy, slide density, audience logic, and transitions.
3. Rehearsal and delivery: timing, emphasis, confidence, room setup, and multilingual presentation friction.
4. Founder-led credibility: Ben's point of view, review process, workshop moments, and practical client-facing judgment.
5. Offer routing: posts that explain when to use the diagnostic, sprint, coaching, or workshop path.

## CTA Pattern

Default CTA:

> Request a free presentation diagnostic and get 3-5 specific fixes for your deck, script, or delivery.

Use softer CTAs on educational posts:

> If the presentation is already live or high-stakes, start with the diagnostic.

Use proof CTAs on before/after posts:

> See the work, then decide whether the diagnostic fits.
