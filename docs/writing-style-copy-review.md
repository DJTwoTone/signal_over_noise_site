## Style Lens Used

Ben's guide points toward copy that is direct, clear, conversational, and a little more alive than standard polished marketing copy. The useful translation for this site is:

- Keep the professional trust and clarity.
- Use contractions and spoken rhythm where it helps.
- Cut generic service-language like "route," "context," and "support path" when plain words work better.
- Add wit lightly, mostly on proof, diagnostic, toolkit, and workshop follow-up copy.
- Avoid heavy sarcasm, profanity, or meme energy on core sales, pricing, and privacy pages.

## Patterns I Would Leave Alone For Now

- Core CTAs like "Request a Free Presentation Diagnostic" and "Request Paid Support" are clear and should stay stable unless you want a broader CTA test.
- Pricing copy should stay practical and restrained. Too much humor there could reduce trust.
- Privacy copy should become more visitor-facing, but should not become jokey.
- The repeated "3-5 specific fixes and a recommended next step" phrase is useful and clear, even if it appears often.
