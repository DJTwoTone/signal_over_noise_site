Paid support FAQ

Before you request paid support

When should I use this form instead of the free diagnostic?

Use this form when you already know you want help, the presentation matters, or the deadline is close. If you are unsure what kind of support you need, start with the free diagnostic.

What should I include?

Include the deadline, audience, format, current materials, and what feels stuck. A deck, script, outline, or practice recording is enough to start.

Will you recommend a smaller scope if that is enough?

Yes. The goal is to match the support to the real problem, not push every request into the biggest package.

What happens to the material I send?

We use your material only to review the request and recommend the next step. If the presentation is sensitive, share a partial deck, sample section, or redacted version.
