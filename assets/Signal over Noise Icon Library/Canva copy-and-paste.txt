ICONS

Use icons to help people scan a route, material, action, or decision. The Signal over Noise icon family is clear, geometric, and restrained. Keep each symbol easy to recognize at a glance and consistent in line weight, proportions, and spacing.

DRAWING STYLE
Use simple outline shapes with rounded line ends and joins. Draw on a 64 × 64 grid with a consistent 2.8 px stroke. Keep interior detail sparse so icons remain clear when reduced. Do not stretch, distort, or combine unrelated icon styles.

COLOR
Use Brand Blue (#0B2D53) or Deep Navy (#132033) for the main outline. Use Warm Gold (#8A641F) for one small, purposeful accent and Light Gold (#F2E5C8) for occasional fills. Use Soft White (#FAF7F2) for reversed icons on deep navy. Do not rely on gold alone to communicate meaning.

SIZE AND SPACE
Use 20–24 px for compact interface icons, 32–40 px for cards, and 48–64 px for larger callouts. Keep the icon proportional and leave an even margin around it. A circle or rounded-square frame is optional; add one only when it helps group or distinguish the icon.

PAIRING AND ACCESSIBILITY
Pair unfamiliar symbols with a short label. Use one icon to clarify each route or action; do not use icons as decoration or as the only way to communicate important information. Give functional icons an accessible name. Hide purely decorative icons from screen readers.

MOTION
Icons are static by default. If motion clarifies an action, use one brief, purposeful reveal. Avoid continuous loops, bouncing, and spinning, and respect reduced-motion settings.

BRAND APPLICATION
Keep the larger Request, Context, Review, and Recommendation illustrations in their own category; they explain process steps and are not interface icons. Reserve the flower-burst emblem for the Signal over Noise logo.
